## Funding context checked so far (October 2026)

Verify each item before quoting it in a proposal.

- **Public health data modernization:** HIMSS lists it as a 2026 policy priority. It also notes that the end of COVID-era relief funding is squeezing state, local and tribal public health. [HIMSS](https://www.himss.org/news-center/policy-news-brief-himss-public-health-digital-transformation-community-advisory-group/)
- **Rural Health Transformation Program:** $50 billion over fiscal years 2026–2030, with workforce and technology among its goals. States apply. [ADvancing States](https://www.advancingstates.org/sites/default/files/2026-01/RHTP%20Applications%20PPT.pdf)
- **CDC Public Health Infrastructure Grant:** funds workforce, systems and data. Its 5-year period runs through November 2027. [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/infrastructure-phig/php/funding-profiles/state-and-territories.html)
- **AI in public health:** one 2026 analysis says dedicated funding is still thin, and points to data modernization streams as a path. **Not verified yet.**
- **"6 in 10":** NACCHO, *2022 National Profile of Local Health Departments*, says 62% of 2,512 departments serve fewer than 50,000 people. This comes from the report text in a search result. The full PDF could not be opened from this environment, so check it there. [NACCHO](https://www.naccho.org/uploads/downloadable-resources/NACCHO-2022-Profile-Report.pdf)
