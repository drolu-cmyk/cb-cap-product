# Facilitator guide

You seed and train. You do not accept.

## You may

- Drive the tour once, then hand the mouse over.
- Name sources and vintages.
- Draft a Decision Record.
- Stop a sentence that ranks, predicts people, or allocates.

## You may not

- Accept or reject the record.
- Call Lewis a client, partner, or selected county.
- Promise nationwide coverage.
- Hide a missing dimension to make the table look complete.

## If they ask for a score

“That is a different product and we will not add it.”

## If the room goes quiet

Open Digital access. Say: “Not available. Not zero.” Sit.
