### Table beats

1. Terrain is terrain. Color is evidence state.
2. Tap 13367. Say lens, not people.
3. Open Digital access. Pale + hatch. Not available. Not zero.
4. Point at the twelve stamps. This is how we refuse a score.
5. Compare. Two dates. Not a ranking.
6. Plan. Amber ring. Read the scenario line out loud.
7. Resource. Four posts. Mismatch. We do not allocate.
8. Draft Decision Record. AI drafts. You accept or reject.

If they only remember one sentence: Missing is not zero.
