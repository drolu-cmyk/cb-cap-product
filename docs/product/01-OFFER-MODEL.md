# CB-CAP — Offer model

## One sentence

CB-CAP is county planning software and a facilitated sprint that turn public and local evidence into a Decision Record a board can defend — without ranking places or predicting people.

## Who pays

Primary buyer: county public health director, hospital planning lead, or a regional planning organization acting for a rural county.

Secondary: state office or philanthropy funding a multi-county cohort.

Not first-screen buyers: pharma brand teams, health plans buying targeting lists, consulting firms buying a white-label.

## Problem

Counties already have CHA/CHIP PDFs, CDC PLACES, HRSA designations, and facility lists. They do not have one table that keeps sources visible, keeps missing data missing, holds twelve access dimensions at once, and produces a dated Decision Record. The usual substitutes — a rank, a travel-time map, a slide deck — collapse the problem and cannot be defended.

## Job to be done

When a county must set or refresh a care-access priority, the owner can open one place, see what is sourced, see what is absent, test a labeled scenario, and leave with a record that names owner, review date, and limits.

## What ships (three SKUs)

### 1. CB-CAP Public Preview — Foundation

Free. Read-only. One demonstration county (Lewis, NY) plus any later public counties. No scenarios that look like forecasts. No Decision Record. Purpose: show the method and attract counties and funders.

### 2. CB-CAP Planning Sprint — Tech Inc.

Eight weeks. One county. Facilitated. Deliverable is a Decision Record pack plus the county workspace seeded from agreed public files and the county’s own CHA/CHIP. Price: project fee (set at proposal). This is the first revenue object.

### 3. CB-CAP County Workspace — Tech Inc.

Annual license after a sprint, or standing license for a cohort. Institutional login. Add local evidence. Compare releases. Draft briefs. Scenario objects remain labeled. Price: annual + support. This is the product that persists.

Do not sell SKU 3 without a source agreement and a named county owner.

## What we do not sell

Closed-loop referrals. Eligibility. Claims. Allocation engines. Composite community scores. Nationwide “ranking of ZIPs.” Autonomous agents that approve plans.

## Economics (working model)

- Sprint pays for first contact and produces the case.
- Workspace is the recurring object.
- Public Preview is marketing and mission, funded by Foundation, built by Tech Inc.
- Dual-cloud later (Pages for public, AWS for institutional) is an infrastructure choice, not a SKU.

## Entity split

| | Tech Inc. | Foundation |
|---|---|---|
| Owns | IP, design system, engineering, commercial offer | Public distribution, mission framing |
| Sells | Sprint + Workspace | Does not sell software |
| Says | Product and method | Public good and access to the preview |

## Success

A county owner can run the 20-minute table without us and produce a Decision Record their counsel will let them keep.
