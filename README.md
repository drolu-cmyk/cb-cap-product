# CB-CAP

County planning for health access.

CB-CAP helps people bring evidence together, examine options, and prepare a record of their decisions. AI can support drafting; people remain responsible for decisions.

The product demonstration uses a synthetic place. Lewis County, New York is a public-data reference only. Neither is a deployment.

## Responsible use

Evidence gaps and assumptions should remain visible. Scenarios are planning tools, not forecasts, and decision records require human review.
