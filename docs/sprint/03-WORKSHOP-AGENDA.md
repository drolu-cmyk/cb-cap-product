# Workshop agenda (three sessions)

Each session: 90 minutes. Owner drives the table after minute 10.

## Workshop 1 — See (week 3)

1. Legend. Color, hatch, marks.  
2. They pick a ZIP lens. Say the lens line.  
3. Open Digital access or Trust. Confirm pale ≠ zero.  
4. Open all twelve stamps. Refuse a score.  
5. Homework: list three documents they will hand over.

## Workshop 2 — Compare and gaps (week 5)

1. Two published dates on one land.  
2. What moved. What stayed Not available.  
3. Draft questions, not answers.  
4. Homework: one labeled scenario in their words.

## Workshop 3 — Scenario and record (weeks 6–7)

1. Read the scenario line out loud.  
2. Resource posts. Mismatch is the object. No allocate button.  
3. Draft Decision Record. Owner edits Owner, date, limits.  
4. Counsel glance if they have one. Accept or reject in week 8.

If time dies, keep: missing is not zero, and the record is not accepted by AI.
